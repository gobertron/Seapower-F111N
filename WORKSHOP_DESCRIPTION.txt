[h1]RAN F-111N Naval Wing — V8[/h1]

An alternate-history Australian naval F-111 family for Sea Power: Naval Combat in the Missile Age. Australia retains fixed-wing carrier aviation and heavily funds a joint RAN/RAAF programme, with successive propulsion, flight-control, radar, EW and weapons upgrades.

[b]Four roles, four editions: 1980, 1985, 1995 and 2003. Sixteen aircraft and 211 selectable loadout presets.[/b] Totals include compatibility aliases with repeated inventories.

[h2]The aircraft[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]F-111N WaterPig — Fleet Defence:[/b] Period IR/BVR missiles, Phoenix and M61 Vulcan. Configured maximum Mach 2.5.
[*][b]FB-111N MudPig — Maritime Strike:[/b] Anti-ship attack, bombing and long-range strike, plus interception, reconnaissance, EW and SEAD options using the other families' dated weapon types. M61 fitted; maximum Mach 2.6.
[*][b]RF-111N SprintPig — Reconnaissance/ELINT:[/b] Defensive missiles, M61, four-tank endurance and clean ReconFast sprint. Fictional Mach 2.52 cruise/Mach 3 maximum. Velocity/thrust response gains are three times matching-year WaterPig; maximum airspeed is not tripled.
[*][b]EF-111N ScreamPig — Electronic Attack/SEAD:[/b] Two physical ECM pods and two offensive ECM systems, defensive missiles, tanks or anti-radiation missiles. Maximum Mach 2.2; no gun.
[/list]
F/FB/RF each have an M61 with 2,000 rounds. MudPig carries removable offensive ECM pods in its EW fits.

[h2]Four funded upgrade blocks[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]1980 — Block I:[/b] Initial naval conversion: AIM-9L, AIM-7F, AIM-54A, Harpoon A, Maverick B, Shrike, Standard ARM, Mk 82/83/84 and Paveway II.
[*][b]1985 — Block II:[/b] Hypothetical TF30 uprating and digital weapons/EW: AIM-9M, AIM-7M, AIM-54C, Harpoon C, Maverick D, HARM A, GBU-15 and Paveway III.
[*][b]1995 — Block III:[/b] Hypothetical F110-GE-400-class adaptation and digital mission suite: AIM-120B, Harpoon D, Maverick G, HARM C, Popeye and SLAM.
[*][b]2003 — Block IV:[/b] Hypothetical F110-GE-129-class adaptation and precision upgrade: ASRAAM, AIM-120C-5, Harpoon Block II, SLAM-ER, GBU-31/32 JDAM and AGM-154A JSOW.
[/list]
Later editions retain earlier bombing options. F/FB/EF afterburning thrust is configured at 82.30/90.53/120/129 kN per engine. Controls, radar/FLIR, RWR/ELINT, ECM, climb, nominal range and weapon readiness improve by edition. RF has a separate fictional propulsion package. These are game settings and estimated integrations, not historical N-family performance.

[h2]Expanded MudPig internal bay[/h2]
[list]
[*]Three Mk 82 bombs or three edition-specific Harpoons.
[*]Two Mk 83/Mk 84 bombs, GBU-12s, Mavericks or Phoenix missiles; two Shrikes in the 1980 Shrike fit.
[*]Two SLAM missiles from 1995; two GBU-31/32 JDAMs in 2003.
[*]44 new bay mission presets for anti-ship attack, bombing, precision strike and interception, including long-range tank fits.
[*]Existing bombing, precision, Maverick, SLAM and JDAM presets gain additional internal stores.
[/list]
The original three stations, concealment and door animations remain. Single-store suspension adapters and interfaces receive estimated mass allowances of 40/50/60/75 kg by edition. Precision/EO control pods stay external; internal Phoenix uses the dedicated air-targeting controller. Popeye, SLAM-ER, JSOW and MER racks stay external. Armed launch, recovery and release require in-game testing.

The original anti-ship fits are retained: [b]Anti Ship[/b] has 2 wing Harpoons and 2 IR missiles with an empty bay; [b]Heavy Anti Ship[/b] has 4 wing + 3 internal Harpoons; [b]Long Range Anti Ship[/b] has 2 wing + 3 internal Harpoons, 2 IR missiles and 2 tanks. Harpoon and IR types follow the edition. The separately relocated gun is fictional and leaves the bay available.

[h2]Late tactical greys[/h2]
Eight 1995/2003 aircraft have US Navy-inspired tactical-grey liveries, Australian roundels, NAVY/A8 lettering, flying-pig badges and subdued markings. Matching tanks and selection profiles are included. Original meshes, cockpit, UVs, protected mechanical areas and landing animations are retained.

[h2]Weight, carrier use and setup[/h2]
Budgets combine F-111C reference data, rounded weapon masses and explicit naval/mission/upgrade estimates. Internal fuel is 14,897 kg; each 600-US-gallon tank adds 1,770 kg fuel plus a 150 kg shell. A full M61 magazine adds 544 kg. All presets are below the 51,845 kg reference takeoff budget; the heaviest is RF-111N 2003 with four tanks at 50,897 kg. This does not certify carrier recovery or catapult limits.

All sixteen aircraft retain carrier capability. The complete installer creates compatibility overrides for discovered stock, RAN and custom carriers while preserving source files and existing air groups. It also supports the existing fictional small-deck arrested-recovery conversion. Add dated aircraft to the carrier air group in the mission editor.

Enable [b]RAN F-111N Naval Wing V8[/b], disable older Naval Wing copies, give V8 priority over carrier mods and keep carrier/source asset mods and the existing Anchor Chain setup enabled. Rerun the complete installer after carrier-mod updates; restart after changing enabled mods.

Service gates are 1980–1984, 1985–1994, 1995–2002 and 2003–2050. Unsuffixed IDs select 1980; later IDs use _1985/_1995/_2003. Existing missions need the later aircraft selected to receive newer equipment. RF ReconFast needs independent orders and suitable altitude; formation orders can limit speed.

[h2]Validation and documentation[/h2]
[b]35,186 mod checks, 2,243 carrier/installer checks and eight late texture audits passed.[/b] Actual gun fire, guidance/release, armed carrier recovery, Mach 3 flight, ECM display and date filtering remain untested in Sea Power.

Full comparisons, every preset and mass budget, CSV/JSON, 61 local store definitions, references, credits, authoring sources and validation reports are included in the [url=https://github.com/gobertron/Seapower-F111N]release repository[/url]. The naval conversion, three-Harpoon bay, F110 adaptations and RF Mach 3 package are fictional. Native JDAM/JSOW, EO, AMRAAM, ASRAAM and Block II Harpoon simulation approximations remain documented.

V8 also includes native store display names, zero-reload chaff, Windows-encoding support, elevator/taxi repairs using existing geometry, and a replacement script with dated backups. Local installation and Steam publication are separate steps; use Update Existing for item 3810606011.
