[h1]RAN F-111N Naval Wing — V7[/h1]

A standalone alternate-history Australian naval F-111 family for Sea Power: Naval Combat in the Missile Age.

In this timeline, Australia retains fixed-wing carrier aviation and heavily funds a joint RAN/RAAF Naval F-111 programme. Successive upgrades improve engine output, flight controls, radar, electronic warfare and weapons integration while preserving four distinct aircraft roles.

[b]Four families. Four editions: 1980, 1985, 1995 and 2003. Sixteen aircraft and 167 selectable loadout presets.[/b] Preset totals include compatibility aliases with repeated inventories.

[h2]The aircraft[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]F-111N WaterPig — Fleet Defence[/b]
Carrier fighter and interceptor with period Sidewinder/ASRAAM, Sparrow/AMRAAM, Phoenix and an M61 Vulcan. Configured maximum: Mach 2.5.
[*][b]FB-111N MudPig — Maritime Strike and All-Rounder[/b]
Anti-ship attack, long-range maritime strike and bombing remain its main jobs. It also carries the other families' period weapon types through interception, reconnaissance, EW and SEAD presets. M61 Vulcan fitted. Configured maximum: Mach 2.6.
[*][b]RF-111N SprintPig — Reconnaissance and ELINT[/b]
Fast reconnaissance with defensive missiles, M61 Vulcan, a four-tank endurance fit or clean ReconFast sprint. Fictional Mach 2.52 cruise and Mach 3 maximum. Velocity/thrust response gains are three times the matching-year WaterPig; maximum airspeed is not tripled.
[*][b]EF-111N ScreamPig — Electronic Attack and SEAD[/b]
Dedicated fleet-support aircraft with [b]two physical pylon-mounted ECM pods and two offensive ECM systems[/b], defensive missiles, and tanks or anti-radiation missiles. Configured maximum: Mach 2.2. No gun.
[/list]
WaterPig, MudPig and SprintPig each carry an M61 with 2,000 rounds. MudPig's offensive ECM pods are removable and used in its EW fits.

[h2]Four funded upgrade blocks[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]1980 — Block I:[/b] Initial naval conversion. AIM-9L, AIM-7F, AIM-54A, AGM-84A Harpoon, Maverick B, Shrike, Standard ARM, Mk 82/83/84 and Paveway II.
[*][b]1985 — Block II:[/b] Hypothetical TF30 uprating and improved digital weapons/EW. AIM-9M, AIM-7M, AIM-54C, AGM-84C Harpoon, Maverick D, HARM A, GBU-15 and Paveway III.
[*][b]1995 — Block III:[/b] Hypothetical F110-GE-400-class adaptation and digital mission suite. Adds AIM-120B, AGM-84D Harpoon, Maverick G, HARM C, Popeye and SLAM.
[*][b]2003 — Block IV:[/b] Hypothetical F110-GE-129-class adaptation and precision-weapons upgrade. Adds ASRAAM, AIM-120C-5, AGM-84L Harpoon Block II, SLAM-ER, GBU-31/32 JDAM and AGM-154A JSOW.
[/list]
Later MudPig editions retain earlier bombing options. Period selection assumes heavily funded Australian acquisition, wiring, software, pylon engineering and trials; some integrations are deliberately accelerated alternate history.

F/FB/EF afterburning thrust is configured at 82.30 / 90.53 / 120 / 129 kN per engine across the four editions. Velocity/thrust response gains rise to 1.10x / 1.25x / 1.40x the 1980 setting; flight-control gains rise to 1.05x / 1.10x / 1.15x. Radar, FLIR, RWR/ELINT, offensive and defensive ECM, climb, nominal range and weapon readiness also improve. RF uses a separate fictional high-thrust package. These are game settings, not measured historical flight results.

[h2]MudPig anti-ship loadouts[/h2]
Harpoon and defensive IR missile variants follow the selected edition.
[list]
[*][b]Anti Ship:[/b] 2 wing Harpoons + 2 defensive IR missiles. Internal bay empty.
[*][b]Heavy Anti Ship:[/b] 4 wing Harpoons + 3 internal Harpoons: 7 Harpoons total.
[*][b]Long Range Anti Ship:[/b] 2 wing Harpoons + 3 internal Harpoons + 2 defensive IR missiles + 2 external fuel tanks.
[/list]
The original three-station internal bay, concealment and door animations are preserved. The separate fictional gun installation leaves the bay available. Laser designation and EO weapon-control pods occupy counted external stations.

[h2]1995 and 2003 tactical greys[/h2]
All eight late editions receive dedicated [b]US Navy-inspired tactical-grey liveries[/b]: darker upper surfaces, intermediate sides and lighter undersides, with Australian roundels, NAVY lettering, A8 serials, flying-pig badges and subdued markings. Tanks and aircraft-selection profiles match. The 2003 scheme adds modest wear.

NAVY and serial lettering is checked on both sides of the aircraft. Original UVs, alpha, protected cockpit/mechanical areas, normal/specular maps, earlier liveries, meshes and landing animations are retained.

[h2]Weight, fuel and carrier use[/h2]
Mass budgets combine F-111C reference data and rounded carried-weapon masses with explicit estimates for naval conversion, gun hardware, mission equipment and upgrades. Each aircraft has 14,897 kg internal fuel. Each 600-US-gallon tank adds 1,770 kg fuel plus an estimated 150 kg shell; a full M61 magazine adds 544 kg.

All presets remain below the 51,845 kg reference takeoff budget. The heaviest is RF-111N 2003 with four tanks at 50,897 kg. This is a mass check, not carrier certification.

All sixteen aircraft retain carrier capability. The release installer creates source-preserving compatibility overrides for discovered stock, RAN and custom carrier decks, including the existing fictional helicopter-deck arrested-recovery conversion. Existing air groups and helicopter/VTOL approaches are preserved. Add the desired dated aircraft to the carrier air group in the mission editor.

Enable [b]RAN F-111N Naval Wing V7[/b], disable older Naval Wing copies, give V7 priority over carrier mods and keep source carrier mods enabled for their assets. Use the complete release installer for generated carrier overrides; rerun it after carrier-mod updates and restart after changing enabled mods.

Service periods are 1980–1984, 1985–1994, 1995–2002 and 2003–2050. Unsuffixed aircraft IDs select 1980; later IDs use _1985, _1995 or _2003. Existing saved missions using old IDs need the later edition selected to receive its newer equipment. For RF sprint, use ReconFast and independent orders at suitable altitude; formation orders can limit speed.

[h2]V7 changes and documentation[/h2]
This is a fresh remake from V6. V7 adds dated propulsion, flight-control and sensor upgrades; improved weapon readiness and estimated AIM-120C-5 ECCM; dated ECM definitions; revised upgrade mass allowances; eight late grey liveries; and expanded comparison, loadout and build documentation.

The release includes all sixteen aircraft comparisons, every one of the 167 presets, mass budgets, sortable CSV/JSON, 61 local store definitions including compatibility entries, historical references, credits, authoring sources and validation reports.

[b]Static validation: 32,062 mod checks and 2,243 carrier/installer checks passed; eight late texture audits passed.[/b] Sea Power runtime testing remains outstanding for gun fire, weapon guidance/release, Mach 3 flight, ECM display, date filtering and actual carrier landings.

The naval conversion, three-Harpoon bay, relocated gun, F110 adaptations, Mach 3 RF package and small-deck recovery are fictional. Native modern-weapon approximations remain: JDAM/JSOW do not add full GPS/INS guidance; EO weapons do not add manual retargeting; Block II Harpoon models ship attack; AMRAAM has no platform midcourse correction and ASRAAM adds no helmet sight/LOAL. The original cockpit is retained.
